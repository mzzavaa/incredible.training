**Purpose.** The format that actually changes how people work, because it runs long enough for new habits to survive the return to normal duties.

**Contains.**
- Weekly rhythm: one live session, one lab block, one office hour, asynchronous review
- Capstone: each participant applies the material to a real problem from their own backlog
- Cohort mechanics: peer review, demo day, and why the group is capped
- Time commitment stated honestly in hours per week
- Manager brief: what the sponsor is expected to protect and unblock
- Completion criteria and what you leave able to do
