**Purpose.** Publishing prices filters buyers before they take up calendar time. This page states what things cost and what changes the number.

**Contains.**
- Three price columns: individual seat, private course, programme
- Price table per course pulled from `data/courses.yaml`
- What is included in every price: materials, lab environment, credential, support window
- What is extra: travel, out-of-hours delivery, bespoke curriculum development, extra assessment attempts
- VAT position and currency handling
- Discounts: group bookings, multi-course bundles, alumni, community, non-profit
- Payment terms and instalments

**Primary CTA.** Check the price for your course, then book.
