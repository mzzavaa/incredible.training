**Purpose.** A lot of training that gets rejected on budget could have been part-funded. This page names the routes and what each requires.

**Contains.**
- Austrian routes: regional and federal continuing education support, and what evidence they ask for
- EU and sectoral funding programmes relevant to digital skills
- Cloud vendor funding: partner-sponsored enablement and when it applies
- What we can supply for an application: curriculum pack, trainer credentials, quote, attendance records
- What we cannot do: we are not funding advisers and do not submit applications on your behalf
- Realistic timelines, because funding decisions are slower than training calendars

**Primary CTA.** Ask which documents you need for your funding application.
