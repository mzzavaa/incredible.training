**Purpose.** Explains how we scale delivery beyond one person without diluting quality, which is the first question a large buyer asks.

**Contains.**
- Associate roster summary by specialism, driven by `data/trainers.yaml`
- Vetting standard: production experience, co-delivery, observed run, sign-off per course
- When associates are used: parallel tracks, large cohorts, specialist modules, other languages
- Quality control: shared materials, feedback scores, and the review cadence
- Substitution policy for private bookings
- Rates and how associate delivery affects a quote

**Primary CTA.** Ask which trainers would be assigned to your programme.
