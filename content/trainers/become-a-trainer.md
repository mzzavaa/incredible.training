**Purpose.** Recruitment page for the associate roster. Aimed at senior practitioners who already speak or mentor and want paid delivery work.

**Contains.**
- What we look for: shipped production systems, ability to debug live in front of a room, patience
- What we do not require: a training certification or a teaching background
- Process: intake call, teach-back session, co-delivery, observed delivery, sign-off
- Commercials: day rate bands, materials licence, travel, and how repeat delivery is allocated
- Support provided: slides, labs, environments, and a co-trainer on your first runs
- Which courses currently need capacity

**Primary CTA.** Apply to join the associate roster.
