**Purpose.** The transparency page. For L&D leads and procurement who need to see the instructional design before they sign.

**Contains.**
- Level definitions: foundation, intermediate, advanced, with observable entry and exit criteria
- Module anatomy: teaching block, lab, checkpoint, reference material
- Minimum lab ratio per level and why we publish it
- Prerequisite graph across the whole catalogue
- Review cadence: every course carries a last-reviewed date and a change log
- Sources: which parts of the curriculum come from published open methodology and which are proprietary
- Accessibility and language: materials, captions, and German delivery on request
