**Purpose.** For anyone who knows the destination role but not the order of courses. Turns a catalogue into a curriculum.

**Contains.**
- Three paths: AI engineer, cloud and platform engineer, technical leadership
- Visual sequence per path: foundation, intermediate, advanced, with prerequisites drawn as edges
- Total time and total cost per path, stated before anyone asks
- Entry diagnostics so an experienced person can skip the foundation stage honestly
- How a path maps onto a team programme rather than an individual plan
- Credential earned at each milestone

**Primary CTA.** Pick the path that matches the role you are hiring for or growing into.
