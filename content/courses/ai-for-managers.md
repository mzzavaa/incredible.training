**Purpose.** You are being sold AI from every direction and asked to sign off on some of it. This
day is about judgment: which proposals are real, what they will actually cost, and which questions
expose a weak one in the first five minutes.

**Contains.**
- What the technology genuinely does well today, and the categories where it still fails, stated
  without either hype or dismissal
- Reading a proposal: the questions that separate a working plan from a demo with a business case
  stapled to it
- Cost, honestly. Why per-task measurement matters, why pilots look cheap and production does not,
  and the arithmetic that changes at volume
- Build, buy, or wait: a framework for deciding, including the cases where waiting is correct
- What a good pilot looks like: scoped, measured, with a stated condition for stopping
- Risk and governance at the level a decision maker needs: data handling, confidentiality, the EU AI
  Act obligations that apply to how you use these systems, and where accountability actually sits
- Adoption as the real bottleneck: why tooling rarely fails and change usually does
- Hands-on, briefly, so the judgments are grounded in having used the tools rather than read about them
- Participants leave with a one-page assessment framework applied to a proposal of their own
