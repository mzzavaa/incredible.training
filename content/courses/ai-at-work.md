**Purpose.** Most people have tried ChatGPT, got a mediocre answer, and quietly went back to doing it
by hand. This day fixes that. For people who want their own week to get lighter, not a lecture on
how large language models work.

**Contains.**
- The honest framing: what these tools are reliably good at, and the three categories of task where
  they still waste your time
- Prompting that survives contact with real work: context first, examples, constraints, format
- Working with your own material: uploading documents, keeping a project going across sessions,
  reusable instructions instead of retyping the same setup
- The four workflows most people get the most from: drafting and editing, summarising and
  extracting, research with sources, and structured thinking on a messy problem
- Verification as a habit: how to check output fast, and which claims to never take on trust
- What not to put in: personal data, client confidential material, anything under NDA
- Each participant leaves with three of their own recurring tasks rebuilt as a repeatable prompt
- Note on tool choice: the day is run on ChatGPT and Claude, and the transferable part is the
  method, not the vendor
