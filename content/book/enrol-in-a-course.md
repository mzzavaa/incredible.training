**Purpose.** The transactional page for individual enrolment. Should take under three minutes end to end.

**Contains.**
- Date picker driven by `data/schedule.yaml` with live seat counts
- Prerequisite confirmation step, with an honest opt-out if the learner is not ready
- Billing details, including the employer invoice option and VAT ID field
- Terms acceptance: cancellation, transfer, and code of conduct
- Confirmation flow: payment, seat confirmation, joining pack, calendar invite
- Accessibility and dietary fields for on-site dates
- What to do if the date you want is full

**Primary CTA.** Complete enrolment.
**Links to.** /for-individuals/seats-and-enrolment/, /schedule/upcoming-dates/, /faq/booking-and-cancellation/.
