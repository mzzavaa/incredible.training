**Purpose.** The main commercial capture form for corporate buyers. Collects enough to produce a real quote without a discovery call.

**Contains.**
- Form fields: organisation, team size, roles, current level, target capability, preferred window, location
- Optional fields that speed things up: existing tooling, cloud platform, procurement constraints
- Course or path pre-selection, if the buyer already knows
- What arrives back: tailored agenda, firm price, proposed dates, trainer names, terms
- Response time and who replies
- Escalation route for procurement deadlines
- Note on confidentiality of anything shared in the form

**Primary CTA.** Send the request and get a proposal in two working days.
