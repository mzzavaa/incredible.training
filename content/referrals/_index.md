**Purpose.** The catalogue here is AI only, taught by one person. Plenty of people arrive needing
something adjacent: the IT groundwork before any of this makes sense, or the DevOps and platform
depth that AI work sits on top of. Sending them to someone genuinely better at it is more useful
than stretching to cover it badly.

**Contains.**
- The stated position: we teach AI, we refer everything else, and we do not take commission
- Referral cards from `data/referrals.yaml`, each with what it covers, who it suits, and the level
- Foundations and DevOps, referred to Techworld with Nana: IT Fundamentals, the DevOps Bootcamp, the
  DevSecOps Bootcamp, and the GitLab CI/CD course
- Cloud platform training, AWS, Azure, and GCP, which used to be in this catalogue and is now either
  referred out or delivered as consulting rather than a scheduled course
- A short note on why each referral is here, written as a recommendation rather than a listing
- What we do not refer: anyone we have not seen the material from
- Cross-link for buyers who wanted delivery rather than teaching

**Primary CTA.** Go to the course that fits, on the partner's own site.
**Links to.** /courses/, /learning-paths/, https://incredible-consulting.com/ for delivery instead of training.
