**Purpose.** Most training budgets are spent on the topic that is fashionable rather than the gap that is blocking delivery. This finds the gap first.

**Contains.**
- Method: self assessment, short practical exercise, manager interview, roadmap review
- Skills matrix: current level per person per capability, anonymised for the report
- Gap analysis against the capabilities the roadmap actually requires
- Output: a costed plan mapping people to courses and paths, with sequence and dates
- Re-assessment option after the programme to show movement
- Data handling: what we collect, who sees individual results, retention period
