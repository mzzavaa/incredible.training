**Purpose.** The commercial front door for learning. One job: move an L&D lead, an engineering manager, or an individual engineer to the right course and then to a booking.

**Contains.**
- Hero: what we teach, who it is for, and the two entry paths (buying for a team, buying a seat)
- Featured course strip from `data/courses.yaml` with code, level, duration, and price from
- Next dates band from `data/schedule.yaml`: three upcoming cohorts or open workshops with seats left
- Formats row: half day, multi day intensive, cohort programme, self paced
- Learning paths teaser: three role-based routes for people who do not know where to start
- Proof band: completion rate, lab hours shipped, named client teams, learner quotes
- Trainer strip with credentials and a link into /trainers/
- Boundary note: training builds people, consulting builds systems, with a link out
- FAQ teaser (three questions) and a closing booking block

**Primary CTA.** Browse the catalogue, or request a quote for a team.
**Links to.** /courses/, /schedule/, /for-teams/, /for-individuals/, /pricing/, /book/, and https://incredible-consulting.com/ for delivery work rather than enablement.
