**Purpose.** The page that converts. Someone has decided they want the course and now needs a date they can put in a calendar.

**Contains.**
- Calendar view and list view of everything in `data/schedule.yaml`
- Per date: course, format, city or remote, language, seats left, price, booking link
- Status labels: open, filling, waitlist, confirmed to run, cancelled
- Filter by city, by remote only, by month, by language
- Waitlist signup for sold-out dates
- Subscribe options: calendar feed and a low-volume dates email
- Note on private bookings, which do not appear here

**Primary CTA.** Book a date, or join the waitlist.
**Links to.** /schedule/upcoming-dates/, /schedule/request-a-date/, /book/enrol-in-a-course/, /courses/.
