**Purpose.** The detailed listing behind the schedule landing page, for people comparing several options at once.

**Contains.**
- Table of every scheduled run from `data/schedule.yaml`, sorted by start date
- Confirmation threshold: how many bookings make a date certain to run, and when we decide
- Cancellation policy if a date does not reach the threshold, including refund and transfer
- Timezone handling for remote cohorts, with the live session times stated in local time
- Venue information for Vienna dates, plus accessibility and travel notes
- Provisional dates marked clearly, with the date they become confirmed

**Primary CTA.** Reserve a seat on a confirmed date.
**Links to.** /book/enrol-in-a-course/, /for-individuals/seats-and-enrolment/, /faq/booking-and-cancellation/.
