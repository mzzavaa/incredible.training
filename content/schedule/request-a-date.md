**Purpose.** Captures demand that the published calendar misses, which is most of the corporate demand.

**Contains.**
- Short form: course, preferred window, group size, city or remote, language
- What happens next: response time, and the threshold at which we open a public date
- Private booking route for groups of six or more, with typical lead time
- Regional interest list: request a course in a city where we do not currently run
- Corporate calendar planning: booking dates six to twelve months ahead
- Note that requesting a date is not a commitment to buy

**Primary CTA.** Send the date request.
