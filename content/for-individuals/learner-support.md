**Purpose.** Sets expectations about support, which is the most common source of disappointment in paid training.

**Contains.**
- During the course: lab support, second facilitator on remote sessions, and pairing
- After the course: office hours schedule, question channel, and response time commitment
- Materials access period and update policy when platforms change
- Alumni channel and demo days
- Escalation: what to do if the course was not what was promised, including the refund position
- Feedback loop: how learner feedback changes the next run of the course

**Primary CTA.** Read the support commitment before you book.
**Links to.** /faq/, /referrals/, and https://incredible.builders/ for the ongoing practitioner community.
