**Purpose.** Keeps the door open for people whose employer is not paying. Explains who qualifies and how the seats are allocated, without making anyone beg.

**Contains.**
- Categories: community organisers, students, career changers, people between roles, non-profits
- Reserved seat count per cohort and how allocation is decided
- Application: a short form, no essay, decision within one week
- Community rate for members of the Incredible community and AWS user groups
- Alumni discount and referral credit
- What is expected in return, stated honestly: attendance, feedback, and nothing else

**Primary CTA.** Apply for a reduced or scholarship seat.
